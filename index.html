How to play

  • Take turns placing one number on the shared grid.
  • Correct: +1. ★ Star cells (the hardest ones): +4. Wrong: −1 and your turn ends.
  • Place the last of any number (the ninth 7): +5.
  • Finish a row, column or box: +3 each and go again.
  • Beat the timer (the board's outline). It gets shorter as the board fills. Leave a line one cell from done and your opponent grabs it. Gold outlines are open goals.
